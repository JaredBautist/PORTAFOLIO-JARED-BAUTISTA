# Page Override: Docencia

This page inherits the Jared Bautista Portfolio master system except where specified.

## Purpose

Present private mathematics and English teaching as a distinct professional service
without weakening the primary software-engineering identity.

## Page Tokens

| Role | Value | Use |
|---|---|---|
| Education sky | `hsl(var(--sky))` | Mathematics surfaces and diagrams |
| Education mint | `hsl(var(--mint))` | English and autonomous-learning surfaces |

## Page Rules

- Preserve Space Grotesk and Archivo; do not introduce childlike display fonts.
- Use Lucide line icons and abstract learning diagrams, not emojis or stock photography.
- Keep one primary CTA per section: contact through WhatsApp.
- Explain AI as guided support for autonomy, never as a replacement for reasoning.
- Present the supplied four years of private-teaching experience in one compact
  credibility block; avoid oversized counters that compete with the main message.
- Do not display invented grade ranges, prices, reviews, or outcome metrics.
- Maintain the existing ES/EN selector and dark-mode compatibility.
